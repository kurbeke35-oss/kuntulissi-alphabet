# Kuntulissi Talking Alphabet (Fara ma Maluwa Muntulissi)

The Kuntulissi alphabet: 22 letters, each with its IPA transcription,
a short description of the sound, and an audio recording.

## Letters and sounds

| # | Letter | IPA | Sound |
|---|--------|-----|-------|
| 1 | A | /a/ | like the Arabic fatha |
| 2 | B | /b/ | b in "ball"; Arabic baa |
| 3 | DH | /ɖ/ | voiced d, tongue high in the mouth, then a pop |
| 4 | E | /e/ | e in "bed" |
| 5 | F | /f/ | f in "fan"; Arabic faa |
| 6 | H | /h/ | h in "hat"; Arabic haa |
| 7 | I | /i/ | ee in "see" |
| 8 | J | /dʒ/ | j in "jam"; Arabic jeem |
| 9 | K | /k/ | k in "kite"; Arabic kaaf |
| 10 | L | /l/ | l in "lamp"; Arabic laam |
| 11 | M | /m/ | m in "moon"; Arabic meem |
| 12 | N | /n/ | n in "net"; Arabic noon |
| 13 | NG | /ɲ/ | Swahili "ny"; Spanish ñ in "año" |
| 14 | O | /o/ | closed o |
| 15 | QH | /ŋ/ | ng in "sing" and "ink" |
| 16 | R | /r/ ~ /ɾ/ | Arabic raa; sometimes like English r in "Royal" |
| 17 | S | /s/ | s in "sun"; Arabic seen |
| 18 | T | /t/ | t in "tea"; Arabic taa |
| 19 | TH | /ʈ/ | t, tongue high in the mouth, then a pop |
| 20 | U | /u/ | oo in "moon" |
| 21 | W | /w/ | w in "water"; Arabic waaw |
| 22 | Y | /j/ | y in "yes"; Arabic yaa |

## Usage

Open `index.html` in a browser, tap any letter to hear it, or press
"Play all" to hear the whole alphabet.
